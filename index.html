01About

I'm a software engineering student at the University of Ottawa, finishing my BASc through the co-op program in December 2026 with a 3.7/4.0 CGPA and a spot on the Dean's List.

Over three co-op terms in the federal public sector and corporate IT, I've built internal platforms and automation that turn slow, manual processes into simple, reliable workflows. I like clear requirements, tight feedback loops with the people who use the software, and shipping things that quietly save everyone time.
